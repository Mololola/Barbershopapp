1 Who we are

Fadra Cut is operated by Luis Guillot Lozano, an individual currently based in Spain (not yet a registered company). For the purposes of the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR, I am the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

You can reach me directly at luis.guillot.lozano@gmail.com for anything in this policy — a small, individually-run operation like this doesn't require a formally appointed Data Protection Officer, but every request below still goes to a real person, not a queue.

2 Who this policy covers

This policy applies to everyone who uses the Fadra Cut mobile app or its business website, whether as a customer booking a service, a business owner running a shop on the platform, or a worker/barber with a profile — in the UK, Spain, or anywhere else. Where a right or rule below applies specifically to one region (the UK vs. the EU/Spain), that's called out explicitly; everything else applies to all users equally.

7 How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, plus a short period where the app's own mechanics require it:

  • If you delete your account (Account Settings → Delete account), deletion is immediate and permanent — your profile, bookings, reviews, posts, messages, and every other record tied to your account is removed right away, not after a waiting period. The one exception is below.
  • Records of reward withdrawals we have already paid to you (the amount, currency, date, your username, and the account holder name and IBAN it was paid to) are kept for 6 years after the payment, even if you delete your account, because tax and accounting law requires us to keep records of money we pay out. Only our payout administrators can see them. Withdrawals not yet paid are deleted with your account.
  • A business account with an active subscription or upcoming confirmed bookings can't be deleted until those are resolved first (so a customer's upcoming appointment doesn't just vanish) — once cleared, deletion is equally immediate.
  • If a business subscription payment fails, the shop stays visible to customers for 3 days, is then hidden until the business pays, and has 60 days from the first failed payment to resolve it before the business and its data are automatically deleted.
  • Stripe retains payment/transaction records for its own legal and accounting obligations, independent of what we delete on our side — typically several years, per Stripe's own policy, since that's a standard financial-recordkeeping requirement neither of us can shorten.

8 Your rights

Whether you're covered by the UK GDPR or the EU GDPR, you have the same core set of rights over your data:

  • Access — ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct anything inaccurate (most of this you can already edit directly in the app).
  • Erasure — delete your account and everything tied to it. This is self-service: Account Settings → Delete account. If you'd rather we do it for you, email us.
  • Restriction — ask us to pause processing your data in specific circumstances.
  • Portability — request a copy of your data in a portable format. This isn't yet a self-service, in-app feature — email us and we'll provide it manually.
  • Objection — object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — for anything based on consent (like Google sign-in), at any time.

To exercise any of these beyond what's already self-service in the app, email luis.guillot.lozano@gmail.com. We'll respond within one month, as required by law.

9 Minors (14–17)

You must be at least 14 to have your own account — below that age, we don't offer accounts at all, in either country. 14 is the age at which both the UK and Spain let a young person legally consent to their own data processing without a parent — so from 14 up, a minor's own agreement (the checkbox at sign-up) is what we rely on, the same as anyone else's. Their account works differently in one respect:

  • A minor's account is restricted, from the moment it's created. Booking, browsing, and leaving reviews all work normally — no payment is involved in booking (see the table above), so there's no money-flow risk there. But a minor's account cannot: send or receive direct messages, post to or comment on the social feed, become a business account, opt into the worker marketplace, or make any in-app purchase. These aren't optional settings — they're built into the account type itself, enforced by the database, not just hidden in the app's interface.
  • Private by default. In line with the UK's Age Appropriate Design Code, a minor's account gets no different public exposure than any other username already gets on this platform (see Section 5) — but with messaging and posting off, there's no way for anyone to initiate unsolicited contact with a minor through the app.
  • Date of birth is self-declared at sign-up, not identity-verified — the same standard every mainstream consumer app uses — and is locked from being edited afterward.

If you're booking a service for someone younger without them having their own account — getting your kid a haircut through your own adult account, for instance — that's always been fine and needs none of the above; this section only applies when the minor holds the account themselves.

10 Security

A few concrete things we actually do, not a generic promise:

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Your session is stored in your device's own secure enclave — the iOS Keychain or Android's encrypted Keystore — never in plain text. On the business website, it's kept in your browser's local storage for this site only.
  • Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider; we never see or store them in readable form.
  • Database access is governed by row-level security policies, so the database itself — not just the app — enforces who can see what.

11 Cookies and local storage

We don't use cookies for tracking or advertising. The mobile app stores a small amount of data on your device itself (your session, so you stay signed in) using your OS's secure storage. The business website keeps the same session in your browser's local storage, which is strictly necessary for signing in and needs no consent banner. That data never leaves your device except to make the requests the app or website needs to function.

12 Automated decisions

We don't make any decisions about you using automated profiling or algorithms that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you. Booking availability is checked against a database constraint that guarantees two people can never be double-booked into the same slot — that's a data-integrity safeguard, not a decision made about you.
