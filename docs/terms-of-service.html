3 What the service is

Barbershop App is a marketplace connecting customers with barbershops and the workers who staff them. Depending on your account type, you can:

  • As a customer: browse businesses and services, book appointments, leave reviews, message businesses and workers, post to the social feed, and design and save 3D haircut concepts.
  • As a business: list your shop, services and pricing, manage bookings and staff, invite and manage workers, and subscribe to unlock the finance planning tool.
  • As a worker: maintain a profile visible to businesses looking for staff, accept or decline job invitations, and manage your own schedule and leave requests.

We're a technology platform connecting the two sides of this marketplace — we are not a party to the actual haircut/grooming service you book, which is a direct arrangement between you and the business.
